Modes are rendering algorithms; presets are ready-made option objects that configure those modes. Use a preset directly or spread it and override any option.

Modes

Dense (full grid each frame): wave, current, ripple, fadeIn, image. Sparse (only active particles/columns are visited — cheaper than a full scan): matrix, snow, sparkle. Custom: set mode:'custom' with a customCell(api) function, or register a reusable one with asciiam.registerMode(name, fn) and reference it by name.

Images and gradients

Images can be loaded from PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP, AVIF, SVG, or any browser-supported image format. Scale controls zoom the source; FOV controls how much of the source is visible. Gradients support linear, radial, and cubic color blending with 1–6 generated steps (2 by default).

Notes

Auto-pauses when scrolled off-screen or when the tab is hidden. Font-size variation is quantized internally and gradients / animated colors / the pulse factor are computed once per frame (never per character) — trail, pulse, and animated color all cost effectively nothing extra.
